FirstPass Image Downloader and Browser

An intuitive image downloader and browser written by a photographer for photographers.

  • Download from several cards or cameras to multiple destinations at once

  • Starts automatically the moment a card or camera is connected

  • Fast, uncluttered “first pass” culling — rate, colour-flag and delete

  • Compare similar frames with synchronised zoom and pan (new in 1.7.1)

  • 0–5 star ratings, nine colour flags and instant rating filters

  • Bulk renaming with date and auto-sequence tokens

  • Available on the Mac App Store

iOS Applications

Location Fix

A simple way to track and share all your favourite locations:

  • Drop a pin or save your current location, with photo, notes, subject and pin colour

  • Live weather in each pin's map callout

  • Standard, Satellite and Hybrid map styles

  • Route with Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze; send places to Tesla or Rivian

  • Siri & Shortcuts — drop a pin or navigate hands-free

  • Collapsible subject groups and a searchable list

  • Share places (with their photo) and save ones sent from other apps

  • Choose your app icon; available in seven languages

  • Automatic iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad

  • Available on the App Store
