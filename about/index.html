About LightOnCanvas

LightOnCanvas is one person: Bhupendra Yadav, a bird and wildlife photographer in Canada who also writes Mac software. FirstPass exists because I wanted a faster, safer way to get a Z8’s worth of frames off the card and down to the keepers — and the tools I found either didn’t verify the copy, didn’t run offline, or wanted a subscription. It’s been on the Mac App Store since 2011 and is rebuilt from the ground up for Apple silicon.

The belief behind the software hasn’t changed: get the design right from the start, so the result is efficient, robust, and stays out of the photographer’s way.
