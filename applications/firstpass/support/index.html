Common questions

FirstPass cannot see my card or camera

Check that macOS has given FirstPass permission to read removable volumes — System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Files and Folders. Cards that mount in the Finder use the Fast path; cameras that do not mount are driven over PTP, which needs the camera switched on and not asleep.

Verification says a file failed

That is the feature working. FirstPass compares a checksum of the source against the copy it wrote, and re-copies anything that does not match. A file that still fails after a retry is reported rather than counted as a success — usually a failing card or a bad reader, and worth knowing before you format anything.

My ratings do not show up in Lightroom

Ratings are not written into your original files — FirstPass keeps them in its own metadata store, so your originals are never rewritten. To carry a rating across, use Export and choose a format other than “Original (copy)” with “Embed star rating in metadata” switched on: the re-encoded JPEG, HEIC, TIFF or PNG carries the original EXIF, capture date, camera and GPS with it, and the rating as XMP that Lightroom reads. Exporting the original file copies the bytes and nothing else. Writing XMP sidecars beside originals, so RAW ratings travel too, is planned.

Can I download from several cards at once?

Yes, and to more than one destination at the same time. That is what FirstPass was built for: plug in every reader you own and let it work through them without swapping cards. Each card gets its own progress and its own Cancel, so stopping one leaves the rest running, and you can browse and rate while they come in.

Why is FirstPass asking permission for my card?

Fast mode reads the card’s filesystem directly, which is considerably quicker than driving the camera, and macOS requires your consent before any application may do that. It is asked once per card and then remembered. This is also why “Start downloading automatically” is unavailable in Fast mode: a grant cannot be given while nobody is watching. Switch Download Mode to Automatic if you want cards to start on their own.

Can I work from an external drive?

Yes. External disks have their own section in the sidebar — expand one to its folders and rate, flag, compare and export straight from it. You can also start the browse tree there instead of in Pictures, under Settings ▸ General ▸ Browse folder, and pin folders on it that come back when the disk does. A disk being written to cannot be ejected out from under a copy.

Where did my transfer report go?

To the destination folder for that job, as a CSV manifest, a PDF summary or an ASC MHL sidecar, depending on what you chose in Download settings.

Reporting a problem

Include these and it will be fixed much faster:

  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected instead

  • Your macOS version and your FirstPass version (FirstPass ▸ About FirstPass)

  • The camera or card reader involved, if it is a download problem

  • The exact wording of any message — a screenshot is ideal

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue one directly, but if something is wrong with the application we would rather hear about it and fix it.
