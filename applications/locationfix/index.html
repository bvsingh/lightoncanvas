Location Fix

Save, organise and share your favourite places

Location Fix keeps all the places that matter to you in one place. Drop a pin, add a photo and notes, organise by subject, and see the current weather right in each pin's callout. Switch the map between Standard, Satellite and Hybrid, route to any place with your favourite maps app, and share them with other people and apps. Drop a pin or navigate to a saved place hands-free with Siri and Shortcuts. Everything syncs across your devices with iCloud. Available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, in seven languages.

Core Features

  • Drop a pin or save your current location, with photo, notes, subject and pin colour

  • Live weather in each pin's map callout

  • Standard, Satellite and Hybrid map styles

  • Route with Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze; send places to Tesla or Rivian

  • Siri & Shortcuts — drop a pin or navigate hands-free

  • Collapsible subject groups and a searchable list

  • Share places (with their photo) and save ones sent from other apps

  • Choose your app icon; available in seven languages

  • Automatic iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad

  • Available on the App Store

What Location Fix Does

Touch and hold the map to drop a pin, or save your current location with a tap. Give each place a name, photo, notes, pin colour and a subject group, then find it again on the map or in the searchable, grouped list.

Key capabilities include:

  • Drop a pin or save your current location

  • Rich detail per location: name, address, photo, notes, subject and pin colour

  • Current weather shown in each selected pin's map callout

  • Switch the map between Standard, Satellite and Hybrid

  • Organise locations into collapsible subject groups, with a searchable list

  • Route with Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze

  • Share to Messages, WhatsApp and maps apps — with the pin's photo — or send to Tesla / Rivian

  • Save places shared from other apps — including geotagged photos

  • Siri & Shortcuts: drop a pin or navigate to a place hands-free

  • Choose your app icon — a glossy coastal pin, the classic map pin, or a dark variant

  • Available in seven languages, with automatic iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad
