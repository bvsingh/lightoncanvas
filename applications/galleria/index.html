Galleria

Photo galleries you own

Import a shoot, arrange it, and publish it to your own server. No account, no monthly hosting bill from anyone but your own host, and your photographs are never uploaded anywhere except where you send them.

What It Does

Most ways of putting photographs online ask you to move in: an account, a subscription to a platform, your images living on somebody else's server, and a gallery that disappears the day you stop paying them. Galleria does the opposite. It builds an ordinary static website — HTML, CSS and your images — and puts it wherever you tell it to.

That means the output is yours. It works from a plain web host, a university server, an S3 bucket, a Raspberry Pi in a cupboard, or a folder on a USB stick. It keeps working if Galleria is never updated again, and it keeps working if you stop using it.

Publishing

Galleria publishes over SFTP straight to your own server. The first time takes as long as your images take to upload. Every time after that it sends only what actually changed — fix a caption on a four-hundred-image gallery and it moves a few pages and no photographs at all.

  • Set a server up once: host, user name, folder, and a password or an SSH key

  • ⇧⌘P publishes to it again with no dialog at all

  • A site is never broken half-way through: images land before the pages that use them

  • Interrupted uploads resume rather than starting again

  • Or export to a folder and put it online however you like

Made For Photographs

  • Colour managed properly, so what you exported is what a visitor sees

  • Responsive images: the browser picks the right size for the screen it is on

  • GPS stripped by default — publishing a nest or a den location is a real harm

  • Your copyright and byline are kept, because stripping those is not privacy

  • Orientation baked into the pixels, so nothing is sideways anywhere

  • Alt text per photograph, and a nudge when a gallery has none

Requirements & Availability

  • Requires macOS 14 or later

  • Universal on Apple silicon and Intel Macs

  • Free to use for 30 days — the whole app, not a crippled version

  • After that: $9.99 a year, or $29.99 once

  • Your galleries stay open and editable whether or not you subscribe, and everything you have already published stays published

  • No account. No telemetry. Nothing is uploaded except a publish you set up yourself.
